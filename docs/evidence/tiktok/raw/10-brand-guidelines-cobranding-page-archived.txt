## Co-branding

The following section sets out when and how to create assets for co-branding and social.

## In a container

Square version for iOS app icon – circular version for Android app icon and Share icon.

## Badge

Use the black circular version as a sticker or badge where relevant to force clear space on partner communications.

## Equally Weighted

Ensure the TikTok signifier is optically the same height as a partner’s logo when they appear together, always use the note without the wordmark.

## Spacing and Sizing

If locking up with other logos, make sure we use correct measurements for spacing and sizing.

## Wordmark

Our primary co-branding lockup uses the TikTok wordmark – ensure there's enough space by using the margins and rules indicated below. Use the orientation best suited to the application.

## Multiple Logos

If there are multiple partnership logos it's best to use our TikTok wordmark in a horizontal format.

## Tag Lockups

Tag lockups should only be used in reference to the TikTok application in partnership with other apps icons. The Tag should always have a black background.

## Do's and Don'ts

When co-branding, it's important to follow these rules to ensure maximum legibility and brand attribution for both the TikTok brand and our partners.

## Too close

Don't position logos too close to the dividing line.

## Unequal sizing

Don't make one logo bigger than the other.

## Change colors

Don't change the colors of logos.

## New lockups

Don't create new types of lockup or partnerships.
