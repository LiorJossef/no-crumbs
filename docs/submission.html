The product

People save the video. What they wanted was the place.

People find restaurants, cafés and bars on TikTok, and they save the video. TikTok keeps that as a video in a list ordered by date — not a place, not a location, not a name. So saving works and getting it back out is broken, and getting it back out is the only moment that matters: you are standing on a street, and the bakery you saved is four hundred videos down.

No Crumbs takes one TikTok link, reads the post on the server, finds the places its caption names, matches them to real coordinates, and — once you confirm them — puts them on your own private map. You reach them by where they are, instead of by remembering when you saved them.

Most videos name the place only out loud or on screen, and we read captions — so roughly three links in four yield no place. That is why “no places in this one” is an ordinary result rather than an error: it has a screen of its own, with three ways forward. The measurement, and how small the sample is, are in the product specification.
