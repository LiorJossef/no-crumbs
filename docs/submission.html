No Crumbs
Submission

A personal map of the places your feed recommended. Paste a TikTok link; the caption is read on the server, the places in it are matched to real coordinates, and — once you confirm them — they land on a map that is yours and stays yours.

The five documents

Each one answers a numbered course requirement, and each is written to be read on its own. This page holds no figures of its own: every measured number lives in the document that measured it, next to how it was measured.

01

Product Specification

What problem the product solves, who it is for, who the customer is, the business goals, the capabilities that meet them, and the core things a person actually does.

M1 · M2 02

Technical Design

The architecture and the detailed design: layers, pages, routes and server actions, the database and its tables, how data moves between browser, server and Postgres, state, errors, validation, and every external service with the reason for it.

M3 · M4 03

Test Specification

What “working” means here, and how it is checked: the core features, invalid input, the central business process, permissions between two real users, the database, edge cases and the interface.

M6 · M7 04

Scale

What happens at tens or hundreds of users, which queries are heavy, which indexes exist, how over-fetching is avoided, where the client/server split falls, and what actually runs out first.

M8 05

Security

How signing in works, how permission is decided in the database rather than in application code, what stops one account reading another’s rows, how input we did not write is handled, where the secrets live — and the risks that remain.

M9 06

Presentation

The 10–15 minute deck, built from the five documents above.
